Hüseyin Özdemir is a film director and screenwriter with a strong interest in visual storytelling. He is Turkish and grew up watching animated films, which helped shape his creativity and unique way of seeing the world.

He is especially interested in exploring themes such as belonging, identity and finding one's place in society. He likes to explore how a person's background, opportunities and privileges can affect their sense of belonging.

His films have been screened at Vega Scene, as well as at several national and international film festivals. As a freelance filmmaker, he is open to different creative projects and collaborations. He enjoys taking part in film events, courses and workshops to improve his skills, learn from others and build connections within the film industry.
